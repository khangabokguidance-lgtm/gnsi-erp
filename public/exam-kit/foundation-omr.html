Each paper's sections and question numbers come from the question booklet. Change anything below and it is remembered in this browser.

Press Use this kit's roll chart to take the rooms and seats from the last roll chart you made in this kit. A CSV from Summary → Download allocation as CSV also works. Each student's sheet then shows their room, seat and exam roll number.

Edit the student list

Single sheet by GCC No.

Type a GCC No. from the ERP. If the student is in the list, the name, class and roll fill in by themselves; otherwise type them. Then print just this one A4 sheet.

Several GCC numbers at once

Type or paste GCC numbers separated by commas, spaces or new lines. Each one gets its own sheet. Numbers not in the list print with the GCC No. only.

Sheets

Paper size: A4 · 210 × 297 mm

Every sheet is exactly one A4 page. In the print window choose Paper size: A4, Scale: 100% / Default (not "Fit to page"), Margins: None or Default, and turn on Background graphics. On a phone, choose Save as PDF and pick A4, then print the PDF.

Answer key

Tap the correct option for each question. The Foundation keys are filled in from the booklet's answer key; the questions with no answer there are left empty for you. The Sainik booklet has no key, so fill it in here. Saved in this browser.

Faster: tap a question, then type A, B, C or D on the keyboard. It moves to the next question. Space skips, Backspace goes back.

Check answer sheets

Pick a student, then enter what they shaded on the sheet: tap bubbles, or tap Q1 and type the letters. Marks are worked out against the answer key straight away. Press Save marks and move to the next student.
