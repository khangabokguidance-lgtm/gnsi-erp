2Students

One student per line: Roll No, Name, Class. Paste from Excel or a CSV, or load a .csv file. A header line is skipped. Any number of classes works. Loaded to start: Sainik Achiever 18, Leader 20, Champion 31: the AISSEE-appearing students, 69 in all.

3Exam roll numbers

Use each student's GCC No as the roll number, or set your own exam roll numbers for every category: a prefix, a starting number and the order. You can also type any single student's roll number by hand below.

4Rooms

Add as many rooms as you need. Rows × Columns is the bench layout seen from the front (Columns = seats across). Capacity defaults to Rows × Columns; lower it to leave seats empty.

5How to seat

Seating plan for each room, as seen by the invigilator facing the class. Print and paste inside the room.

One roll chart / attendance sheet per room. Print blank for students to sign, or tap P / A on screen to mark attendance (saved on this device for this exam date), then print or download the marked sheet.

Big notice for the door of each room, listing the roll numbers seated inside.

Room-wise counts and an alphabetical list for the notice board, so every student can find their room and seat.
