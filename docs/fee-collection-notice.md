# URGENT NOTICE — FEE COLLECTION

**To:** Fee Counter Staff only
**From:** Management
**Date:** ____ October 2026
**Subject:** Mandatory fee collection by 10 November 2026

Outstanding student fees have reached a level the institute can no longer carry. Fee collection is now the **top priority** for the fee counter staff.

**Instructions**

1. Fee counter staff must collect all outstanding fees **on or before 10 November 2026**.
2. Follow up with every defaulting parent or guardian by phone, in person and in writing. Use the Month-wise Dues ledger in the ERP to see who owes what.
3. Submit your **signed collection report** (students contacted, amount collected, amount still pending with reasons) at the **Founder's Office by 10 November 2026, 5:00 PM**.
4. Every receipt must be entered in the ERP on the day of collection. Reports that do not match the ERP will not be accepted.

**How to collect, step by step**

Open the ERP → **Help Center** → **Fees** → "Fee counter routine: collect dues step by step". In short:

1. Student Fee Ledger → **⚠️ Month-wise Dues**: pick the month and print your call list.
2. Contact each parent. Fees → **Reminders** opens a WhatsApp dues message.
3. Fees → **Fee Payment**: search the student by GCC no., choose the fee, check the amount, set date and mode, collect and print the receipt.
4. Part payment: collect it with a reason, or agree a plan in Fees → **Instalments**.
5. End of day: Fees → **Day Close**, count the cash and save the closing.
6. Report: Student Fee Ledger → **🧾 Month-wise Collection**, pick the month, print, sign and submit at the Founder's Office.

**Timeline**

| Date | Action |
|---|---|
| 27 Oct 2026 | Dues lists issued to the fee counter staff from the ERP; collection drive starts |
| 3 Nov 2026, 5:00 PM | Progress check: report to the Founder's Office on the amount collected so far |
| 7 Nov 2026 | Final reminders to all parents and guardians who have not paid |
| 10 Nov 2026, 5:00 PM | **Final deadline:** fees collected and signed collection report submitted at the Founder's Office |

**Consequence of non-compliance**

No extension will be given. Fee counter staff who have not collected the dues assigned to them and have not submitted the collection report by the deadline will have their **salary held** until the report is submitted and reviewed by the Founder's Office.

This notice is final. Treat it as urgent.
